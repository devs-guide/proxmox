## Proxmox VE support windows by Debian base

Proxmox VE tracks its upstream Debian base; using the latest stable release is recommended. Support generally follows the Debian lifecycle (~3 years from release). Reference the matrix below when choosing a target:

| Proxmox VE | Debian (codename) | First Release | Debian EOL | Proxmox EOL |
|------------|-------------------|---------------|------------|-------------|
| 9          | 13 (Trixie)       | 2025-08       | tba        | tba         |
| 8          | 12 (Bookworm)     | 2023-06       | 2026-08    | 2026-08     |
| 7          | 11 (Bullseye)     | 2021-07       | 2024-07    | 2024-07     |
| 6          | 10 (Buster)       | 2019-07       | 2022-09    | 2022-09     |
| 5          | 9 (Stretch)       | 2017-07       | 2020-07    | 2020-07     |
| 4          | 8 (Jessie)        | 2015-10       | 2018-06    | 2018-06     |
| 3          | 7 (Wheezy)        | 2013-05       | 2016-04    | 2017-02     |
| 2          | 6 (Squeeze)       | 2012-04       | 2014-05    | 2014-05     |
| 1          | 5 (Lenny)         | 2008-10       | 2012-03    | 2013-01     |

For this repository’s 0.0.1 RC, the supported target is Proxmox VE 6.4 (Debian 10/Buster). Newer Proxmox releases should prefer the latest stable branch.
